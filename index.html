Nurse Staffing Warehouse
legendary
What it does

A star-schema warehouse over 2,625,134 daily nurse-staffing records covering 14,589 US nursing homes, with a React dashboard on a FastAPI serving layer.

What makes it different

It rebuilds the quarterly average CMS already publishes from the daily payroll source and reconciles to it at r = 0.999996 before making any new claim. It also separates 2,789 non-reporting days from genuine zero-coverage days, a distinction that would otherwise overstate the headline figure by 27%.

Why it is useful

36.5% of facility-days fall below the 2024 federal minimum, and weekends fail at 61.4% against 26.6% on weekdays. 1,291 of 5,232 facilities rated 4 or 5 stars on staffing are below the minimum on at least a quarter of their days, so a family reading that star rating is reading something the daily data contradicts.

Claim Check
legendary
What it does

An agent that fact-checks a claim written in plain English against a 485M-record warehouse and streams back a verdict.

What makes it different

The language model never writes SQL. It picks a measure and a dimension from a closed catalog, so every identifier reaching the query is a validated catalog key and literal values travel as bound parameters. A deterministic judge decides the verdict, so the model never rules on truth.

Why it is useful

Against a 20-claim gold set it produced zero confidently-wrong answers at 1.00 abstention recall. Removing the scope guard made it answer three questions the data cannot address, which is the behaviour the guard exists to prevent. 59 tests cover it, none of which load a language model.

Clinical Trial RAG
epic
What it does

A running retrieval service over 114,900 ClinicalTrials.gov oncology trials that streams generated answers token by token.

What makes it different

A deterministic guardrail checks every trial identifier in a generated answer against the documents actually retrieved. Across the evaluation set that produced zero fabricated citations, at a grounded rate of 1.00.

Retrieval runs at 13.2 ms mean latency with 0.867 Precision@5, packaged as a multi-stage Docker image running as a non-root user, with CI covering lint, a 15-test suite and a real image build. It is deployable rather than a notebook.

Claims Denial Recovery
epic
What it does

A business-analysis decision package built on 485,167,265 commercial health-insurance claims from CMS Transparency in Coverage filings.

What makes it different

It deduplicates issuer totals repeated across 16,444 plan rows. Without that step the national denial rate is weighted by how many plans an issuer files rather than by claim volume. 31 issuer-years filing internally impossible values were flagged and excluded, and one of them alone moved the national appeal-overturn rate by 6.8 percentage points.

Why it is useful

20.6% of claims are denied and only 0.26% are appealed, while 31.9% of appeals succeed. 47.0% of denials are process failures against 4.6% for medical necessity, which moves the business case away from appeals and toward front-end prevention. The ROI model returns 2.74x in the base case and states where it stops working: below 60,000 claims a year.

Trial Site Selection
rare
What it does

A 3NF schema over 40,000 oncology trials and 115,077 deduplicated investigator sites, measuring competition between trials through a temporal interval self-join on shared sites and indications.

What makes it different

The effect is conditional, not general. Competitive density raises accrual-failure risk 31% relative for single-site trials, from 7.93% to 10.41% at z = 4.49, and has no effect once a trial spans multiple sites. That only appeared after narrowing the outcome from all-cause termination to accrual-specific failure.

Why it is useful

It tells a sponsor when site crowding is worth screening for and when it is not, instead of applying one check to every trial. Getting there meant excluding 59,548 sponsor placeholder records, 15.5% of the site data, which would otherwise have dominated the rankings.

Enrollment Risk Screening
rare
What it does

An eight-document decision package for adding competitive-density screening to clinical trial site selection: business case, BRD, process maps, KPI framework, RACI and risk register.

What makes it different

It is grounded in the z = 4.49 finding measured in the project above rather than assumed inputs, and it bounds the intervention to the roughly 35% of trials with fewer than three sites, because no effect was measurable past that. Most of the portfolio is scoped out on evidence instead of given screening friction for no benefit.

Why it is useful

The cost-benefit model is runnable and returns 1.13x ROI with a 10.3-month payback in the base case. It also reports the scenario where the programme loses $40,948, rather than leaving that column out of the sensitivity table.

Supply Chain Risk
common
What it does

A normalised model of the transitive dependency graph of 117 widely used npm packages, traversing 6,174 edges with a recursive CTE and path-based cycle detection to reach 1,985 distinct packages.

What makes it different

It measures what actually installs rather than what a manifest declares. Direct dependency counts understate real exposure by up to 71 times: jest declares 4 packages and installs 284. Risky-dependency rate also does not fall with smaller dependency trees, which is the assumption a manifest audit rests on.

Why it is useful

52.8% of reachable packages have a single maintainer, and 17.1% are both single-maintainer and more than two years without a release. That is exposure a direct-dependency audit misses entirely.

Cold Chain Monitor
common
What it does

Firmware, an ingest API and a SQL compliance layer for cold-chain storage monitoring, classifying temperature excursions across 8,580 simulated readings over 72 hours.

What makes it different

Gaps-and-islands run detection with a configurable grace period reduces 68 raw out-of-range runs to the single reportable 170-minute event, separating a sustained equipment failure from 67 transient door openings with a median duration of 3 minutes.

Why it is useful

Mean Kinetic Temperature per ICH Q1A comes out at 5.487 °C against an arithmetic mean of 5.157 °C, capturing the Arrhenius weighting an average throws away. It also caught a 60-reading hole in the record that a completeness check would have passed. The physical device was never assembled, so every figure here is from a labelled simulator.
