OPTIONAL — AMBIENT VIDEO LOOPS
══════════════════════════════

Want a screen to breathe? Drop a short loop here named after its screen:

  01-hero.webm   (+ 01-hero.mp4 as a fallback)
  06-achievements.webm
  ...

Then add a `loop` field to that screen in src/config/site.config.js.
See SETUP.md → "Adding video loops".

Recommended: 5-6 seconds, 1280x720, no audio, under 1.5MB.

⚠️ Animate 3-4 screens at most. If everything moves, nothing stands out.

Generation prompts + the ffmpeg commands: MOTION.md at the project root.
